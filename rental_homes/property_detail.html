About this space

Designed by acclaimed Balinese minimalist architects, Villa Balian merges sustainable teakwood craftsmanship with clean modern lines directly fronting the unspoiled surf haven of Balian Beach. Wake to uninterrupted waves breaking gently against black sand shores, surrounded by private coconut groves.

The villa includes a daily dedicated gourmet chef offering organic farm-to-table Balinese breakfasts, full housekeeping, and an on-call concierge. Relax inside the double-height vaulted timber pavilion, rejuvenate within the outdoor lava stone soaking tub, or dive into the 18-meter saltwater infinity pool perched over the tide line. High-speed Starlink WiFi seamlessly covers every corner of the property.
